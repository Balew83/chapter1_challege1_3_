06 / About

Perception is an active process.

Gestalt psychology describes common ways people organize visual information into patterns and meaningful wholes. These principles can help designers communicate relationships and hierarchy, but they are not rigid rules about how every person must see a design.

Use the examples in this lab as a starting point: change one visual relationship, compare, and notice what becomes easier or harder to perceive.
